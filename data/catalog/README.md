# Catalog v1

Twenty bounded exercise metadata records, authored for this project's offline,
synthetic general-wellness planning experiment. No third-party routine,
instruction text, images, proprietary content or personal records were imported.
Generic movement names are used; provenance is recorded on every record.
This document makes no external license grant or broader repository license claim.

## catalog-v1

Eight bodyweight repetition records, eight bodyweight timed records, and four
dumbbell repetition records. All are tagged for the two software experience bands.
Compatibility tags are product constraints, not professional judgments of personal
exercise suitability. Dumbbells-and-bodyweight permits bodyweight-only plans;
there is no requirement for dumbbell conditioning records. The catalog size is
driven by inspectable coverage, not by generating near-duplicate records to fill
a quota. Source of truth: `v1.json`; the earlier F1-01 provenance fixture is not
loaded as a real exercise.

Metadata was inspected for IDs, equipment/mode/goal consistency, exclusions,
provenance and contract coverage during F1-02. Independent qualified human content
review has **not** occurred and remains required before any participant trial.
No exercise instruction, load, clinical adaptation or suitability assurance is
provided. Prescription numbers and the four-seconds-per-repetition constant are
contract-12 scheduling constraints, not universally recommended exercise doses.

IDs and tags are explicit. Unknown exclusions never become ignored constraints;
an exhausted catalog yields clarification or abstention. Repetition across days
is allowed. Version changes require a new catalog artifact/revision and a new
evaluation manifest. Do not silently relabel v1 data after freezing results.

The local evaluation report records the canonical catalog SHA-256 and all eight
rows' eligible counts and three-preset feasibility. Coverage is conditional on
exclusions and the fixed metadata; it is not a physical-safety or training-outcome
claim.
