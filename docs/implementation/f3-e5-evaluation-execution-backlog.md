## Concrete human-review allocation proposal

Resource-minimum recommendation for owner approval: R1 covers all20 pairs;
R2 covers predeclared even-numbered pairs2,4,...,20 under the fixed dev-then-
independent pair mapping (five development/five independent shared pairs). These
are role placeholders, **not appointed people or actual judgments**. Prefer at
least one rater outside implementation; declare all developer/curator conflicts.

This plans60 variant reviews and180 applicable dimension slots (3 dimensions per
review), including missing/abstained/ineligible. Shared agreement has20 matched
outputs clustered in10 cases, not40 independent people. Two raters on all20 pairs
is a stronger alternative:80 variant reviews/240 slots. Owner-only review is a
weaker disclosed alternative, not independently reviewed human quality evidence.

Operator keeps case/split/model/variant/reviewer keys private; raters see randomized
A/B eligible outputs/preferences and the existing anchored rubric, not source,
usage, price, failure-origin or hidden reasoning. Balance orientation and review
order before collection; a seed is not proof of successful blinding. Preserve
raw ratings/disagreements, adjudication only as an additional record. Quality
analysis is descriptive: per-dimension applicable counts/means/medians and paired
wins/ties/differences, conditional on both outputs being eligible and rated.
Keep all40 hard outcomes and every planned slot alongside those conditional means.
Do not impute missing scores, score nonexistent explanations or infer effectiveness.
